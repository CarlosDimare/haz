Define a new facet (personality dimension) for Ojito. Use this when you detect a recurring theme, domain, or type of task that the existing facets (hacer, decir, saber) don't adequately cover. Each facet has a unique icon (single Unicode character), a color (one of: warning, info, success, error, primary, secondary, accent), and a list of keywords that trigger it. New facets are saved to .opencode/facets.json and become active immediately on next render.
